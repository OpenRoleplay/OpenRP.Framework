# Locations

`Shared/Locations/Helpers/ZoneNames.GetZoneName(position)` names the San Andreas area a position lies in: Los Santos, San Fierro, Las Venturas, or the county around them (Red County, Flint County, Whetstone, Bone County, Tierra Robada). Outside these, it's "San Andreas".

**Used by:** [Emergency calls](../features/emergency-calls.md), for the area a 911 call comes in from.

## Known limitations

- **Cities and counties only:** no districts such as Idlewood or Ganton yet.
